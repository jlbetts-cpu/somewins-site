Some More Wins and tips

Some More Wins and tips are bought through your Apple Account. To cancel a plan, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. To ask for a refund, use reportaproblem.apple.com. To bring a purchase back on a new phone, tap Restore Purchases in the app's Settings.

Your wins and your data

Your wins live on your phone and in your own iCloud. To move to a new phone, sign in to the same Apple Account and turn on iCloud for Some Wins. How everything is kept is in the privacy policy.

Crews and safety

You can report or block anyone from their profile or from a message, and leave any crew at any time. Reports come to me.
